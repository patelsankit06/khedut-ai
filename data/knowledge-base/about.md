## Capabilities

Khedut AI is a smart farming assistant that answers questions about crop stages, soil requirements, irrigation, fertilizers and nutrient deficiencies, diseases, pest management, and harvesting/post-harvest handling. It answers using a curated agriculture knowledge base and always cites the source it drew from, so you can see exactly where an answer came from. It currently does not take voice input, analyze uploaded photos of plants, or speak Hindi or Gujarati - it answers in English based on the written knowledge base.

## How to Use Khedut AI

Select a crop from the sidebar (Pomegranate, Tomato, Wheat, Cotton, or Potato) to get answers focused on that crop, or leave no crop selected to ask a general farming question across all crops. Type your question in the chat box at the bottom and press Ask. Each answer includes numbered source citations at the bottom of the message showing which guide and category (for example, Irrigation or Diseases) the information came from. You can also upload your own PDF, DOCX, TXT, or Markdown documents from the sidebar as additional reference material - these are searchable regardless of which crop is selected.

## Example Questions

Here are the kinds of questions Khedut AI is built to answer:
- "My pomegranate leaves are turning yellow, what could it be?"
- "What fertilizer is recommended for tomato during flowering?"
- "How often should I irrigate a wheat field?"
- "What are the symptoms of bacterial blight?"
- "When is the right time to harvest cotton?"
- "What soil type is best for growing potatoes?"
- "How do I manage pests on my pomegranate crop?"

## Supported Crops

Khedut AI currently covers five crops in its knowledge base: Pomegranate, Tomato, Wheat, Cotton, and Potato. For each crop it covers crop stages, soil requirements, irrigation, fertilizers and nutrients, diseases, pests and pest management, and harvesting and post-harvest handling.

## Limitations

Khedut AI only answers from its knowledge base and will say so plainly if a question falls outside it, rather than guessing. For safety, it will not name a specific pesticide, fungicide, or fertilizer brand or dosage unless the knowledge base explicitly provides it for that exact situation - instead it describes the general approach and recommends consulting a local agricultural extension officer or expert before applying any chemical. Disease-related answers describe "possible causes" based on symptoms, not a confirmed diagnosis.
